King’s College London

Targeting Elevated Intracellular Sodium and Metabolic Reprogramming in Heart Failure

My PhD investigated how elevated intracellular sodium alters cardiac metabolism, and whether lowering sodium could help reverse metabolic reprogramming in heart failure. Using isolated hearts, cell models and optogenetic tools, I explored the relationship between sodium homeostasis, mitochondrial function and cardiac energetics.

ARIA Precision Mitochondria

Part of a project within the £66M Precision Mitochondria programme, developing tools to engineer the mitochondrial genome.

Led by Prof Hansong Ma at the University of Birmingham, the project aims to develop ways to deliver genetic instructions into mitochondria, express them and maintain them over time, enabling new approaches to studying mitochondrial function.
