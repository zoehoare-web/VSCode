Grant funding

I have experience in research grant management as part of the International Programmes team at the British Heart Foundation, where I contributed to the management of the following programmes.

CureHeart

An international research programme developing genetic therapies for inherited heart muscle diseases. CureHeart aims to correct or silence disease-causing genes, with the goal of preventing or curing genetic cardiomyopathies.

AI in Cardiovascular Health Grand Challenge

A British Heart Foundation initiative bringing together expertise across disciplines to harness artificial intelligence for cardiovascular health. The challenge aims to transform how heart and circulatory diseases are predicted, prevented, diagnosed and treated.

Wellcome Leap VISIBLE programme

A programme focused on improving the diagnosis and treatment of coronary microvascular disease in women with stable angina. VISIBLE aims to develop scalable diagnostic tools and treatments informed by the underlying mechanisms of disease.
