Interests

Ideas, questions and interests beyond individual research projects.

Solving aging by fixing mitochondrial dysfunction

The importance of bioenergetics in age-related disease is clear, but the tools to develop a cure do not exist. Building the bioenergetic measurement layer could unlock drug discovery.

Working on building the technology to solve this through the Fifty Years VC 5050 programme.

Improving sustainable energy technologies

Obtained a Level 3 Award in the Rational Use of Sustainable Energy in association with the 2041 Foundation.

Injury repair & tissue regeneration

As a competitive netball player, I know the lifelong impact of joint injuries. While no FDA-approved drugs can reverse osteoarthritis, advances in cell and gene therapies offer hope - stem cell therapies have been approved in Korea since 2012 and are now in phase 3 clinical trials in the US.
